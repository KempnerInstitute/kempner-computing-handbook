# Trustworthy Agentic Research

This chapter covers using agents in research in a way you can stand behind. {doc}`Evaluating and Monitoring Agents <evaluating_and_monitoring_agents>` keeps an agentic workflow honest by checking whether its output is right, what it actually did, and what it cost. {doc}`Agentic AI in Research <agentic_ai_in_research>` covers where agents fit in the research lifecycle, common workflow patterns, best practices for trustworthy results, research integrity and compliance, and the pitfalls to watch for.
